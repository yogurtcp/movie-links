What these rankings mean

Ranked by IMDb rating, among non-adult movies, series and miniseries with at least 50,000 votes in our 15 October 2026 snapshot. This view includes all 4,674 movies and 721 series/miniseries before the graph’s median-rating cutoff. Some titles therefore have no mapped connections yet.

Genre percentile = 100 × (titles rated lower + half of titles with the same rating) ÷ titles in that genre and format. Within one genre and format, the percentile follows the IMDb score; it is not an additional quality score. Every title counts equally.

Equal IMDb ratings share the same rank and percentile. Vote totals only order tied titles on screen. Series and miniseries share one group. Genres overlap; each genre is ranked separately. Groups with fewer than 30 titles are marked “small group.” These rankings reflect audience ratings, not a prediction that you will enjoy a title.
