IMDb score vs release year.

Two graphs covering all rated movies and series in our snapshot, with no minimum vote count or rating cutoff. Each colored cell counts titles in one year with the same score. Brighter colors mean more titles; the logarithmic color scale makes both sparse and crowded areas visible. The graphs use the same axes and color scale.

345,326 movies and 139,880 series/miniseries are plotted. For series, release year means the year the show premiered. Snapshot: 15 October 2026.

Scope and how to read these graphs

Every title counts once, regardless of its vote total. The 50,000-vote and median-rating rules used for the connection graph do not apply here. Both charts use the same color range, so a color represents the same number of titles in either chart. A blank cell has no titles.

The full snapshot contains 345,360 rated movies and 139,913 rated series/miniseries. Of these, 34 movies and 33 series have no known release year and cannot be plotted. Unrated titles cannot appear on a score axis. As in our original distribution, this scope covers non-adult IMDb types movie, tvSeries and tvMiniSeries; individual episodes, shorts, TV movies and other title types are excluded.

Recent years are incomplete, especially 2026. Low-vote titles can have unstable ratings. More titles in recent years also reflect coverage and production volume; these charts alone do not establish that titles have become better or worse.
