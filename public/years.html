YEARLY AVERAGES

Average IMDb score by year.

Each point shows the average IMDb score of titles released that year: add their ratings and divide by the number of titles. Every title counts equally, regardless of its vote total. There is no minimum vote count, score cutoff or smoothing.

Separate graphs for 345,326 movies and 139,880 series/miniseries, with matching axes. For series, year means the year the show premiered. Snapshot: 15 October 2026.

Scope and how to read these graphs

The average is calculated separately for each year and format: sum of title IMDb ratings ÷ number of rated titles. Ratings are not weighted by vote totals. The 50,000-vote and median-rating rules used for the connection graph do not apply. A gap in the line means that year has no rated titles; no values are filled in.

The full snapshot contains 345,360 rated movies and 139,913 rated series/miniseries. Of these, 34 movies and 33 series have no known release year and cannot contribute to a yearly average. Unrated titles have no score to average. As in our original distribution, this scope covers non-adult IMDb types movie, tvSeries and tvMiniSeries; individual episodes, shorts, TV movies and other title types are excluded.

Years with few rated titles can fluctuate sharply. The CSV downloads include each year’s average and title count. Recent years are incomplete, especially 2026, and low-vote title ratings can be unstable. These are present-day ratings grouped by release year, not ratings recorded at the time of release.
