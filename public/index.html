From a first map to a bigger one

This prototype asks whether named, explorable connections are more useful than a generic list of similar titles. The catalog has 12 descriptive dimensions for every title. The graph offers different routes; choose a dimension for a closer comparison.

What is real here?

The titles are real. Their trait profiles are editorial interpretations made for this prototype. A connection means the two profiles share a phrase. The ranking favors more distinctive shared phrases; it is not a probability or a prediction of your rating.

What makes a bigger map useful?

A much larger catalog, source-backed descriptions, confidence for each trait, and feedback on which connections actually work for you. A rating threshold should consider vote counts and allow exceptions for niche titles. Series need season-level descriptions where they change substantially.

Useful existing pieces

Cinnamon DB describes 20 emotional dimensions for films and shows. Criticker recommends from users with compatible ratings. MovieLens Tag Genome 2021 contains 1,084 tags for 9,734 movies. None of those datasets powers this prototype.
